Understanding TENS Therapy and Its Applications

An educational guide to transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation.

Category: TENS Therapy

What Is TENS Therapy?

Transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation, commonly known as TENS, is a therapy technique that uses mild electrical impulses delivered through electrodes placed on the skin. It is commonly used as part of pain management approaches under appropriate professional guidance.

How Does TENS Work?

A TENS device sends controlled electrical impulses through electrodes attached to the skin. These impulses stimulate nearby nerves. The sensation may feel like gentle tingling, depending on the device settings and individual response.

Common Applications of TENS

TENS is used in different pain management settings. Its suitability depends on the individual's circumstances, the type of pain and professional assessment.

Understanding TENS Equipment

TENS equipment generally includes a control unit, connecting lead wires and skin electrodes. Different devices may offer different programmes and adjustment settings. Users should follow the manufacturer's instructions for electrode placement and device operation.

Safety and Professional Guidance

TENS may not be suitable for everyone. People with implanted electronic devices or particular medical conditions should seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional before using electrical stimulation equipment. Avoid placing electrodes in areas where the manufacturer advises against their use.

Conclusion

TENS is an established electrotherapy approach used in pain management. Understanding its basic operation, equipment components and safety considerations can help readers become more familiar with this technology.
