About Us

Welcome to Therapy Equipment Guide, an independent educational platform focused on physiotherapy, rehabilitation technology and medical equipment.

Our aim is to make information about therapy technologies easier to understand. We publish educational articles covering TENS therapy, neuromuscular electrical stimulation (NMES), EMG biofeedback, pelvic floor rehabilitation and electrotherapy accessories.

We aim to help readers learn about equipment features, common applications, maintenance considerations and rehabilitation technology.

Our Editorial Approach

We value clear explanations, useful educational information and responsible content. Our articles are intended for general information and are not a substitute for professional medical advice.
